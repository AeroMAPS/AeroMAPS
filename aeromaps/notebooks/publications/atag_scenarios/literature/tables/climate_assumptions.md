*Non-CO₂ assumptions, by band. The quantities the climate analysis varies, at each of its three bands. The bands are named for the climate outcome they produce rather than for the magnitude of the parameters, so the low-warming band carries the smallest contrail forcing together with the strongest mitigation, and reading down the avoidance rows the gain decreases. The first two rows multiply: the band is set on one standard deviation of their product and split between them in proportion to the variance each contributes, which is why neither is at an extreme of its own literature range. The particle number emission index is not an assumption in its own right but the number that reproduces the row above it under the square root relation the model uses. The three avoidance families are run as separate variants rather than together, the reports including none of them.*
