*Demand and price in comparable studies. How comparable studies let demand respond to the cost of decarbonising, grouped by approach, extending the comparison of Costa Alves et al. (2026) with the roadmaps the reports themselves cite before setting the question aside. The price is not the same quantity across rows, a fuel price, a fuel cost per unit of demand and an airfare reaching the traveller differently, so the elasticities are comparable in sign and order of magnitude rather than cell for cell. Neither are the demand effects on one basis: each cell states whether it is a change against the work's own uncoupled counterfactual, a change in a growth rate, or a share of an emissions reduction. The last row is this work, whose two figures are the fixed-share mandate and the no-SAF counterfactual it is drawn against.*
